## 🎯 What Is Strata?

Strata is a magical tool that turns audio into living, breathing terrain. Imagine a topographic map—the kind you see for mountains and valleys—but it moves and changes shape based on the music you play. When the bass drops, the mountains swell. When beats ripple, the contour lines dance. Every creation is unique, shareable, and visually stunning.

This isn't just a screensaver or static image generator. Strata creates **live, interactive 3D landscapes** that react to any sound input on your computer. Whether you're listening to Spotify, watching YouTube, or playing your own files, Strata visualizes the audio in mesmerizing ways.

Everything you see can be shared. Look for a URL or token display on the screen—it automatically updates to represent your current landscape. Copy that link and send it to anyone. When they open it, they'll see the exact same topographic design (though movement will follow their own audio).

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### "The page seems frozen" or "Nothing is moving"
- Make sure music is actually playing and not paused.
- Check that your browser volume isn't muted.
- Try refreshing the page.

### "My computer says WebGL isn't supported"
- This is rare. Update your browser to the latest version.
- If using Chrome, go to `chrome://settings` → System → check "Use hardware acceleration when available."
- Update your graphics drivers from your computer manufacturer's website.

### "The download didn't work"
- Ensure you have enough disk space.
- Try a different browser if the download fails repeatedly.
- Disable any download blockers or antivirus temporarily (re-enable after).

### "Audio is very quiet" or "No reaction"
- Increase the volume on your device and in your music player.
- Some keyboards have a mute button that may be pressed—check for an orange or red light on your keyboard.
